No approved locations loaded.

5. Location History

History is recorded while GPS tracking is running and at least one sharing session is active. Owners and approved viewers can load the route while the session remains active.

History recording uses a minimum interval of about 30 seconds, or a movement threshold of about 100 metres. GPS availability and browser operation affect recording.
